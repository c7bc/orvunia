Face Landmarker model bundle, float16, version 1
The upstream BlazeFace, FaceMesh V2 and Blendshape V2 model cards each identify their model license as Apache License, Version 2.0.
See PROVENANCE.json for the versioned download URL, exact hashes, and model-card evidence URLs.

Cadence adds a local worker, iris-relative geometry, temporary nine-point calibration,
ridge regression, signal rejection and smoothing. MediaPipe Face Landmarker itself
does not supply a calibrated screen-gaze estimate. These additions do not identify people.

Google's general MediaPipe Tasks privacy notice permits usage/performance metrics.
Cadence's dedicated worker restricts fetch, XMLHttpRequest and importScripts to
same-origin /assets/gaze/ files and disables WebSocket/EventSource. The application
must also serve a restrictive CSP (connect-src 'self', worker-src 'self').
Frames, facial landmarks, calibration features and coefficients stay in memory.

No Cluely or InterviewCoder source code is included in this implementation.
