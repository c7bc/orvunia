## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final symbol for an independent open-source
desktop AI conversation assistant named Orvunia. A premium minimalist identity
by a senior identity designer, precise geometric construction, immediately
readable at 16px. Single centered flat symbol only, NO text or letters or wordmark.
Original design: an open rounded diamond/lens assembled from two broad opposing
bent ribbons, one in deep jade #126C50 and one in fresh leaf green #63B77A. Their
negative space suggests a calm conversation opening and focus, without literal
chat bubbles or microphones. Bold balanced silhouette, slight asymmetry through
a clean diagonal opening at upper right, generous internal space. Two solid
colors only, sharp vector-like edges, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no
mockup, no canvas/background. Transparent background with generous padding.
Avoid fingerprints, asterisk/sparkle symbols, generic AI stars, competitor logos
and branding. Must look distinctive and restrained, suitable for a professional
Windows application icon and a small website wordmark symbol.
